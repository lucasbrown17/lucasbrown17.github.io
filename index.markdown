> We may have knowledge of the past but cannot control it; we may control the future but have no knowledge of it.
> Claude E. Shannon

I am engaged by real world problems where complex behaviour modelling, mathematical control and software/hardware implementation intersect to give robust, stable and adaptable solutions. 

I'm a Control Systems Software Engineer at the UK Atomic Energy Authority, the country's leading fusion energy research centre. I design and implement novel control techniques for fusion device application and robotic remote maintenance systems. 

*Interests:* Control Theory, Machine Learning, Real-Time Control & Data Acquisition Implementation.
